Looking Glass turns your phone into a simple digital magnifier locked to roughly 10x
magnification.

On phones that have a dedicated telephoto camera, Looking Glass automatically selects
that lens so most of the magnification is optical, keeping the image sharp. On
phones without a telephoto lens it falls back to the main camera and reaches 10x
digitally, up to the hardware limit.

Features:
* Full-screen live camera preview at ~10x zoom
* Automatically uses the telephoto lens when your device has one
* On-screen readout of the active lens and the optical x digital zoom breakdown
* Works fully offline — no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no tracking

Looking Glass collects no data. The camera feed is processed on-device, in real time,
and is never recorded or transmitted.

Requires the Camera permission to show the preview.
